# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

Our laboratory combines Drosophila and mouse models to address two central themes — **regulation of energy metabolism** and **mechanisms of aging** — through an integrated program of mechanistic dissection, target discovery and translational application:

- **Obesity research**: genome-wide screens to identify regulators of energy metabolism (MTCH2, NMI, TRPC6, SEC16B and others); dissection of the neural mechanisms linking sweet taste and nutrient sensing; development of natural-compound and combination therapies.
- **Aging research**: a Drosophila lifespan experiment system and NAD-pathway anti-aging drugs; mechanisms of mTORC1 and neuronal aging.

{% include section.html %}

## Obesity Research

Obesity is a major risk factor for hypertension, cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes. Using Drosophila as a model organism, we perform genome-wide functional screens to identify novel regulators of energy metabolism and validate their function in mammals with knockout mice, aiming to discover new anti-obesity drug targets. We also study the relationship between sweet taste perception and energy metabolism.

## Aging Research

We have developed a Drosophila lifespan experiment system based on 3D printing and computer algorithms, improving experimental efficiency 17-fold, and are developing anti-aging drugs that target the NAD pathway.
