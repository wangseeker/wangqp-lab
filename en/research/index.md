# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

Our laboratory uses Drosophila, mouse models and human genetic data to address two central themes — **regulation of energy metabolism** and **mechanisms of aging** — through an integrated program of mechanistic dissection, target discovery and translational application.

We focus on three connected questions:

<div class="question-grid">
<div class="question-card"><span class="question-num">01</span><h3>How does nutrient sensing drive energy imbalance?</h3><p>How are sweet taste, non-nutritive sweeteners and nutritional geometry sensed and converted into feeding and metabolic signals?</p></div>
<div class="question-card"><span class="question-num">02</span><h3>How do we discover targets and regulatory networks?</h3><p>How can genome-wide screens turn obesity GWAS loci into validated, druggable targets?</p></div>
<div class="question-card"><span class="question-num">03</span><h3>How do we achieve durable therapeutic intervention?</h3><p>How can small molecules, antibodies, siRNA and circular mRNA-LNP deliver tissue-specific, sustained effects?</p></div>
</div>

<div class="pipeline">
<div class="pipeline-step"><span class="step-num">01</span><strong>Human genetic data</strong><p>GWAS and weight-loss cohorts nominate candidate genes</p></div>
<div class="pipeline-step"><span class="step-num">02</span><strong>Drosophila functional screens</strong><p>Genome-wide RNAi/CRISPR for rapid validation</p></div>
<div class="pipeline-step"><span class="step-num">03</span><strong>Mouse functional validation</strong><p>Tissue-specific knockouts reveal physiology</p></div>
<div class="pipeline-step"><span class="step-num">04</span><strong>Candidate drug discovery</strong><p>Small molecules, antibodies and mRNA-LNP for translation</p></div>

Two research lines:

- **Obesity research**: genome-wide screens to identify regulators of energy metabolism (MTCH2, NMI, TRPC6, SEC16B and others); dissection of the neural mechanisms linking sweet taste and nutrient sensing; development of natural-compound and combination therapies.
- **Aging research**: a Drosophila lifespan experiment system and NAD-pathway anti-aging drugs; mechanisms of mTORC1 and neuronal aging.

## Obesity Research

Obesity is a major risk factor for hypertension, cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes. Using Drosophila as a model organism, we perform genome-wide functional screens to identify novel regulators of energy metabolism and validate their function in mammals with knockout mice, aiming to discover new anti-obesity drug targets. We also study the relationship between sweet taste perception and energy metabolism.

## Aging Research

We have developed a Drosophila lifespan experiment system based on 3D printing and computer algorithms, improving experimental efficiency 17-fold, and are developing anti-aging drugs that target the NAD pathway.
