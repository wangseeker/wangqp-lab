# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-hourglass-half" %}Aging Research

Aging is the single largest risk factor for chronic diseases including metabolic disorders, neurodegenerative diseases and cancer. Using Drosophila as a classical model organism, combined with systems biology and medicinal chemistry, we study **the mechanisms of aging and the development of anti-aging drugs**, organized along three lines: lifespan experiment systems, NAD-pathway drugs, and mechanisms of lifespan regulation.

## Core Questions

- Which pathways control lifespan and healthspan?
- How can lifespan experiments be made faster and larger?
- How can the NAD pathway become tissue-specific, durable drugs?

## Drosophila Anti-Aging Experiment System

Drosophila has a short life cycle, high homology with humans and is easy to manipulate, making it a classical model for lifespan research. However, lifespan experiments are long and laborious, and cannot be run at large scale in parallel, which limits progress in aging research.

We developed a Drosophila lifespan experiment system based on **3D printing and computer algorithms**, consisting of three parts: **a food bulk-replacement device, an imaging device, and computer analysis software**:

- Prototype testing showed a **17-fold improvement in experimental efficiency** compared with conventional methods
- Computer-based fly recognition eliminates tedious manual counting and frees researchers from repetitive work
- **Fills a gap in equipment for this field**

## NAD-Pathway Anti-Aging Drug Discovery

Cellular NAD levels are maintained mainly through the **NAD salvage pathway** (about 90%). Raising NAD levels can delay aging. We have:

- Screened a **virtual library of 400,000 natural-product molecules** and identified and validated a novel small-molecule agonist that raises NAD levels; structure optimization is ongoing
- Developed **circular mRNA delivery of NAMPT**: a single intravitreal injection of lipid nanoparticles carrying NAMPT-encoding circular mRNA protects against dry age-related macular degeneration (AMD) — enabling tissue-specific supplementation of the NAD pathway (*J Control Release* 2026)
- Established a **systematic pipeline for developing targeted therapeutic agents** based on CRISPR screening (*Nature Protocols* 2024)

## Mechanisms of Lifespan Regulation

- **mTORC1 pathway**: RagC phosphorylation autoregulates mTOR complex 1 activity — a core node linking nutrient sensing and aging
- **Neuronal aging**: neuronal Lamin regulates motor circuit integrity, motor function and lifespan
- **Nutrient sensing and lifespan**: nutritional geometry reveals how protein–carbohydrate balance affects sleep and metabolism (collaboration, *Nature Metabolism* 2019)
- **SIRT1 and atherosclerosis**: integrative systems biology identifies SIRT1 as a senescence-related therapeutic target, with poliumoside as a natural activator (*Frontiers in Pharmacology*)
